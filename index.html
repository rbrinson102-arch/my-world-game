How it works

  • The life: gangs, mafia families, cartels and syndicates. Every network breaks the rules for gain. Pick yours in Empire.
  • Come up: rob stash houses, run jobs, deal at the Trap, scam, smuggle. Hire a crew and build your organization.
  • Own it: take blocks at the corner flag, take houses, tax shops. One owner per house and block.
  • Take from players: if they're online, put them down or catch them locked up first. Offline, you fight their shooters. Rookies under 3,000 score are safe until they attack.
  • Run the city: you need 3+ blocks. The seat changes hands only by death, jail, 3 days gone, or the boss losing their corners.
  • Sit-downs: meet other bosses in Empire. Leave as partners or rivals.
  • Heat: stars bring cops, SWAT at 4. Break line of sight, hide at your house, or respray your car. Hit Backup (B) and your people pull up.
  • Jail is real minutes: lawyer, public defender, bail, escape or snitch. After time served you're on paper.
  • Cops: rise from patrol to the CIA, clean or crooked.
  • Endgame: wear The Crown, go legit, come back a Legend.
