How it plays

  • Pay Pookie for intel and the door card shows who's inside before you walk in.
  • Get away: break line of sight and the stars drop. Hide out at a house you own, or respray your car at Sparkle Car Wash. At 2 stars the cops shoot; at 4, SWAT. Shoot back or run.
  • Turf war: walk to a block's corner flag and start a war. Clear the soldiers, hold the block 20 seconds, and it pays you. Bosses can call sit-downs in Empire: make peace, pay for it, declare war, or hit a truce partner's block behind their back.
  • Cops start easy and get tougher as you rank up. Lose them by breaking line of sight, shoot their cars until they wreck, hide out, or respray.
  • Drive-by: shoot from your car (aim with the mouse, or FIRE auto-aims on phones). Iron Works sells assault rifles, the street sweeper and pipe bombs (G or BOMB).
  • Get busted and you go to county jail for real minutes. Hire a player lawyer, use the public defender, post bail, or play your second character while you wait. Lose your case? Put a hit on your lawyer.
  • Anyone can work as a lawyer on the side (Empire → pass the bar). Travel between Raleigh, Durham, Charlotte and Chicago from the map.
  • Escort and Pimp are playable lives now. Escorts take dates from their phone and dodge vice stings. Pimps recruit, protect their people and keep the cut fair, or people walk.
  • Locked up? Try to escape (35%), or have another player bust you out from the precinct (40%).
  • Stash your cash at any property you own. Getting busted or killed can't touch the stash.
  • Every day you get 3 jobs worth $750 each, plus a $1,500 bonus for finishing all 3.
  • Your progress backs up online. Your save code is in Empire: type it on any phone or computer to keep playing where you left off.
  • Climb Corner → Runner → Lieutenant → Boss → Kingpin → The Crown. Tap Ranks to see where you stand against everyone.
  • Walk into a stash house, clear the rooms, crack the safe, get out, lose the cops.
  • Iron Works sells pistols, SMGs, shotguns, armor and ammo.
  • Cops chase at 1 star, shoot at 3. Busted costs 40% of your cash, Wasted 20%.
  • Cop mode: ram the getaway car, then hold the action button to cuff whoever runs.
  • Six ways to start: gang member, smuggler, grower, club owner, dancer or cop. Each one has its own story.
  • Cops who die lose 10% of their cash, 5 rep, the call they were on, and their cruiser gets towed back to the precinct.

Play with friends

  • Send your friends the link. Anyone who opens it on a phone or computer shows up in your city, live. No account needed.
  • Every player wears a tag: street name, crew, rank (Boss gets a crown, cops get their badge rank) and wanted stars.
  • Shoot it out with other players. Take out a wanted player for a bounty. Cops can cuff wanted players.
  • Tap Talk to say something. It pops up over your head for everyone nearby.
  • Come back daily: your streak pays more each day, up to $1,750.
