How it works

  • The life: gangs, mafia families, cartels and syndicates. Every network breaks the rules for gain. Pick yours in Empire.
  • Every lane goes to the top: gang member, smuggler, grower, club owner, dancer, escort, pimp. Each has its own 6-rank ladder to The Crown and its own leaderboard (Ranks → My lane).
  • Come up: rob stash houses, run jobs, deal at the Trap, scam, smuggle. Hire a crew and build your organization.
  • Own it: take blocks at the corner flag, take houses, tax shops. One owner per house and block. Leave your houses unguarded while you're offline and someone can take them.
  • Take from players: if they're online, put them down or catch them locked up first. Offline, you fight their shooters. Rookies under 3,000 score are safe until they attack.
  • Every night is different: Monday Crackdown, Tuesday Shipment (first to unload it at the docks, 8 to 11 PM Eastern, gets $10,000), Wednesday Payday, Thursday Fight Night, Friday Club Night, Saturday Turf Night, Sunday Crown Night. Tap the 🔥 button.
  • The weekly crown: every Sunday at 9 PM Eastern, whoever holds the most of the city (blocks, houses, shops, the boss seat) wins $25,000. Standings and street news are in Ranks.
  • Information is money: escorts and dancers take dates with real players, get paid up front, and can sell the date (where and when he'll be alone) to whoever wants him. Escorts, dancers and pimps can sell where anybody they got close to is right now. Nobody holds a city alone: allies make everything you own harder to take, and holding a lot with no allies makes you weaker.
  • Rats: snitching heats you up. Get exposed and everybody sees 🐀 over your head, deals warn people, and gangsters get paid for putting you down (not your own crew). Hot rats can take witness protection to an empty city and build there, until somebody buys intel (crooked fed, lawyer, or street seller), the target goes on the board, and crews fly in to finish it. Kill the rat there and that city opens to everybody.
  • Conquer and expand: the boss of a city who holds 6 blocks or 5 houses can open the next city for everybody: Goldsboro, Raleigh, Durham, Charlotte, Chicago, then the rest of the country and the world.
  • Run the city: you need 3+ blocks. The seat changes hands only by death, jail, 3 days gone, or the boss losing their corners.
  • Sit-downs: meet other bosses in Empire. Leave as partners or rivals.
  • Heat: stars bring cops, SWAT at 4. Break line of sight, hide at your house, or respray your car. Hit Backup (B) and your people pull up.
  • Jail is real minutes: lawyer, public defender, bail, escape or snitch. After time served you're on paper.
  • Cops: rise from patrol to the CIA, clean or crooked.
  • Endgame: wear The Crown, go legit, come back a Legend.
